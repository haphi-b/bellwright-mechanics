# (Neutral) Villages
These interpretations about 'Neutral Villages' are based on [Villages (data)](../data/villages.data.md).

- It's possible to increase the total head count of an NPC village by 2 via building village houses there (via player building menu).

## Possible player interactions
- With carts/caravans villages buy from other villages or your markets. This is possible even before liberation or having any trust with them. [TODO: opens the question if liberation or higher trust improve/change trade prices?]


## Prosperity
### Effects of higher prosperity*
- Villages increase NPC quality by shifting to more villagers with 2-3 stars and less low/no star NPCs.
- Increasing prosperity by one stage gives a village an additional cart for their trading caravans. At prosperity 1 they start with 1 and ultimately can reach 5. 


### How to improve prosperity
- To increase prosperity it’s normally sufficient to just sell a town the things that are red in their economy overview tab. So you don’t necessarily buy stuff from them.
